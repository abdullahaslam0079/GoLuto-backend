For Arooj

A letter, in the shape of a film

Arooj

from your husband

WAIT WAIT WAIT

Who is the love of Abdullah’s life?

Scene I

I didn’t wait for a special day.

The calendar didn’t ask me to do this.

Scene II

I made one.

Because you make ordinary days feel like they belong to us.

Still frames

A life I love.

Scene III

Because.

  • Because the room changes when you walk into it.
  • Because home isn’t a place until you’re in it.
  • Because I still look for you, even when I already know where you are.
  • Because loving you is the easiest true thing I know.
  • Because of the way you exist — fully, quietly, completely you.
  • Because the best days of my life have your face in them.
  • Because I chose you. And I keep choosing you.

A letter

Arooj,

I don’t have a reason, and that is the point. Not an anniversary. Not a birthday. Not a day anyone told me to remember you.

Just a day I looked at my life and thought — she should know. You should feel, for a few minutes, how large you are in it.

You are the quiet I come home to. The person who turns ordinary rooms into something that belongs to us. I don’t say it enough. I wanted you to have something you could open, and keep, and feel.

I think of the day we met before the ceremony — just us, talking, trying to understand the life we were about to begin. Then our Nikah: white, gold, your hand in mine. Barat — you in red, a new chapter opening. And Walima, when the celebration finally had a room of its own. Then the mountains — our honeymoon, the best time of our life. Just us, and a sky big enough to hold it. And our first Eid together — a blessing that finally had your face beside mine.

Of all the lives I could have had, this is the one I want — the one with you in it.

I love you.

Always,
Abdullah

The last frame

Arooj

I love you.
